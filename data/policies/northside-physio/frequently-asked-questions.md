# Frequently Asked Questions

## Common questions

Do I need a referral to see a physiotherapist? No. You can book directly without a referral. You may still need one for your insurance to pay, so check your plan.

How many visits will I need? It depends on the injury. Most patients with a sprain or strain see improvement within four to six visits. Your physiotherapist will give you an estimate at your assessment.

Can I see the same physiotherapist every time? Yes, we book you with the same physiotherapist unless you ask to switch.

Do you offer dry needling? Yes, dry needling is part of our acupuncture service and costs the same.

Do you sell braces and supports? We sell knee braces, ankle braces and back supports at the front desk. Custom orthotics are made by a chiropodist we refer to.

Do you do concussion care? Yes, two of our physiotherapists are certified in concussion rehabilitation. Book a 60-minute concussion assessment for $140.

Is there a pool for aquatic therapy? No, we do not offer aquatic therapy, but the community centre next door runs public aquafit classes.
