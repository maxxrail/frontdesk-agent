# Your First Visit

## Initial assessment

Your first appointment is a 60-minute initial assessment. Your physiotherapist will review your history, assess your movement and strength, and build a treatment plan with you.

## Forms

Please arrive 10 minutes early to complete intake forms on a tablet at the front desk. If you have a doctor's referral or imaging reports, bring them with you.

## Follow-up visits

Follow-up treatment sessions are 30 or 45 minutes, depending on your plan.
