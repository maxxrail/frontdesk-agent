# Cancellation Policy

## Notice required

Northside Physio requires 48 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment. You can reply to your reminder text, call us, or use the online booking portal.

## Late cancellation and no-show fee

Cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice, and missed appointments, are charged a $40 fee. The fee cannot be billed to insurance or WSIB.

## Exceptions

We waive the fee once per calendar year, no questions asked. After that, exceptions are made only for medical emergencies.
