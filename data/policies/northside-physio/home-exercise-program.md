# Home Exercise Program

## Your program

After each visit you get a home exercise program through our app, with videos of every exercise. You can also ask for a printed copy.

## How often

Most programs take 15 to 20 minutes a day. Doing your exercises between visits is the best predictor of how quickly you improve.

## If an exercise hurts

Mild discomfort is expected. If an exercise causes sharp pain or pain that lasts more than two hours afterwards, stop doing it and tell your physiotherapist at your next visit.
