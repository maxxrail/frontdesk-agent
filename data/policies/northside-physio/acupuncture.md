# Acupuncture

## Who provides it

Acupuncture is provided by physiotherapists with additional certification in contemporary medical acupuncture.

## Is it safe

We use single-use sterile needles. Tell your therapist if you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, or take blood thinners, as acupuncture may not be suitable.

## What it feels like

Most people feel a brief pinch, then a dull ache or heaviness. Sessions usually last 20 minutes.
