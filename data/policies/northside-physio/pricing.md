# Prices

## Physiotherapy

Initial assessment, 60 minutes: $125.
Follow-up treatment, 30 minutes: $90.
Follow-up treatment, 45 minutes: $110.

## Massage therapy

Registered massage therapy is $95 for 45 minutes and $125 for 60 minutes.

## Acupuncture

Acupuncture added to a physiotherapy session is $25. A stand-alone acupuncture session is $80.

## Taxes

Physiotherapy is HST exempt. Massage therapy is HST exempt when provided by a registered massage therapist.
