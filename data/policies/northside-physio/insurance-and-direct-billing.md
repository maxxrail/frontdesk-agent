# Insurance and Direct Billing

## Direct billing

We direct-bill Manulife, Sun Life, Green Shield, Canada Life, Blue Cross and Equitable Life. Bring your plan card to your first visit so we can set up billing.

## Doctor's referral

Many insurance plans require a doctor's referral for physiotherapy coverage. Check your plan before your first visit; we cannot submit a claim your plan will reject.

## Coverage limits

Your plan may have an annual maximum. Ask us to check your remaining coverage at any time.

## OHIP

Physiotherapy at Northside Physio is not covered by OHIP.
