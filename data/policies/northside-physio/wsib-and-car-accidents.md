# WSIB and Motor Vehicle Accident Claims

## WSIB

We treat injured workers with active WSIB claims. Bring your claim number to your first visit. WSIB patients pay nothing out of pocket for approved treatment.

## Car accidents

For motor vehicle accident injuries, we bill your auto insurer under the Minor Injury Guideline. Bring your claim number, the adjuster's name, and the insurer's contact details.

## Paperwork

We complete the treatment plan forms your insurer needs. Please allow three business days for forms that are not part of a regular visit.
