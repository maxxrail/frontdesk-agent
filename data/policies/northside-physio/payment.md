# Payment

## Accepted payment

We accept debit, Visa, Mastercard, and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash or American Express.

## When to pay

Payment is taken at the end of each visit. If we direct-bill, you pay only the amount your plan does not cover.

## Card on file

We ask for a card on file when you book. It is charged only for late cancellation fees, after we text you first.
