# New Patient Visits

## Before your first visit

New patients receive a link to our online intake form when they book. Please complete it at least one day before your appointment. The form asks for your health history, current medications, and insurance details.

## What to bring

Bring photo identification, your insurance card, and a list of any medications you take. If you have recent dental X-rays from another clinic, we can request them with your consent.

## How long it takes

A new patient exam takes about 90 minutes and includes a full examination, X-rays, and a cleaning if time allows.
