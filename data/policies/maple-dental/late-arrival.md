# Late Arrivals

## Grace period

We hold your appointment for 15 minutes past the scheduled start. If you arrive within the grace period we will do as much of the planned treatment as time allows.

## Arriving after the grace period

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your visit, and the late cancellation fee may apply. Hygiene appointments are the most time-sensitive because cleanings are booked back to back.

## Running late

If you know you will be late, reply LATE to your reminder text so the team can plan around your arrival.
