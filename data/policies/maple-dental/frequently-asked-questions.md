# Frequently Asked Questions

## General questions

How often should I get a cleaning? Most adults should have a checkup and cleaning every six months. Patients with gum disease may need a cleaning every three or four months, and your hygienist will recommend a schedule.

Do you see patients with dental anxiety? Yes. Let us know when booking and we will schedule a longer appointment, explain each step before we do it, and agree on a hand signal so you can ask us to pause at any time. Nitrous oxide is available for $60 per visit.

Can I bring my child to my own appointment? Children can wait in the reception area only if another adult is with them. Our team cannot supervise children during your treatment.

Do you offer Invisalign or braces? We offer clear aligner treatment after an orthodontic consultation, which is free. Traditional braces are referred to an orthodontist we work with.

Is there wifi? Free guest wifi is available. The password is posted at the front desk.

Can I get a sports mouthguard? Custom sports mouthguards are $150 and take one week to make after your impression appointment.

Do you treat seniors in long-term care? We do not offer visits at long-term care homes, but we can recommend a mobile dental service.
