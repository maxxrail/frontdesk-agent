# After an Extraction

## The first 24 hours

Bite on gauze for 30 to 45 minutes. Do not rinse, spit forcefully, smoke, or drink through a straw for 24 hours, as this can dislodge the clot and cause a dry socket.

## Pain and swelling

Use a cold compress on your cheek for the first day. Over-the-counter pain relief is usually enough; follow the dosage on the package or the instructions your dentist gave you.

## When to call us

Call the clinic if bleeding has not slowed after four hours, or if pain gets worse after the third day.
