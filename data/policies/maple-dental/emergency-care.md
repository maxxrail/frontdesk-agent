# Dental Emergencies

## What counts as an emergency

Severe tooth pain, swelling of the face or jaw, a knocked-out tooth, or a broken tooth with sharp edges are dental emergencies.

## Same-day emergency slots

We keep two emergency slots open every weekday morning. Call the clinic as soon as we open at 8 a.m. to request one. Emergency slots are not bookable by text message.

## After hours

When the clinic is closed, call our main number and choose option 9 for the on-call dentist. For swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

## Knocked-out tooth

If a permanent tooth is knocked out, hold it by the crown, not the root, keep it moist in milk, and call us right away. The best outcomes happen within one hour.
