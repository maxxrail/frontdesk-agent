# Records and Privacy

## Your records

You can request a copy of your dental records and X-rays. We provide digital copies within 10 business days. There is no charge for digital copies sent to another dental office.

## Who can see your information

We share your information only with your consent, with your insurer for billing, or when required by law.

## Text messages

Our reminder texts never include treatment details. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages.
