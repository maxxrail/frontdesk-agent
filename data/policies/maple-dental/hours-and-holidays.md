# Hours and Holidays

## Regular hours

Monday to Thursday: 8 a.m. to 7 p.m.
Friday: 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.
Saturday: 9 a.m. to 2 p.m., every second Saturday.
Sunday: closed.

## Holidays

The clinic is closed on all Ontario statutory holidays and between December 24 and January 1.

## Weather closures

If we close because of severe weather, we text every affected patient by 7 a.m. and rebook them at no charge.
