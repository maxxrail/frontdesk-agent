# Insurance and Billing

## Direct billing

We direct-bill most major insurance providers, including Sun Life, Manulife, Canada Life, Green Shield, Desjardins and Blue Cross. With direct billing, you pay only the portion your plan does not cover.

## Plans we cannot bill directly

Some smaller plans and health spending accounts do not accept direct billing. In that case you pay in full at the visit and we give you a receipt to submit yourself.

## Canadian Dental Care Plan

We are a registered provider for the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP). Bring your CDCP member card to your appointment. Some procedures need pre-approval from the plan before we can book them.

## Pre-treatment estimates

For treatment over $500, we can send a pre-treatment estimate to your insurer so you know your coverage before you commit. Estimates usually take one to two weeks.
