# Prices: Exams and Cleanings

## Regular checkup and cleaning

A regular adult checkup with cleaning is $185 before insurance. This includes the exam, scaling, polishing and a fluoride treatment if requested.

## Children

A child's checkup and cleaning, for patients under 14, is $120 before insurance.

## X-rays

Bitewing X-rays are $65 and are usually taken once a year. A full set of X-rays is $160 and is usually taken every five years or at a new patient exam.

## Fee guide

Our prices follow the provincial dental association fee guide, which is updated every January.
