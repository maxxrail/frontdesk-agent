# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment

We accept debit, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and e-transfer. We do not accept cheques or cash over $200.

## Payment plans

For treatment over $1,000 we offer payment plans through a third-party financing partner, subject to approval. Ask the front desk for an application.

## When payment is due

Payment for your portion is due at the end of each visit. Balances older than 60 days may be sent to collections after two written reminders.
