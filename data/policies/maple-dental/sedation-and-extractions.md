# Before Sedation or an Extraction

## Fasting

If you are having oral sedation, do not eat or drink anything except water for six hours before your appointment.

## Getting home

You must arrange for an adult to drive you home after sedation. You cannot drive, take a taxi alone, or use transit by yourself for the rest of the day.

## Medications

Tell us if you take blood thinners. Do not stop any medication unless your physician tells you to.
