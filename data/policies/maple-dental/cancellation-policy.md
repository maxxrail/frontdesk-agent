# Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

## Notice required

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Notice can be given by replying to your reminder text, calling the front desk, or using the link in your confirmation email.

## Late cancellation fee

Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged a $50 late cancellation fee. The fee is not billed to insurance and must be paid before your next visit.

## Missed appointments

If you miss an appointment without any notice, a $75 missed appointment fee applies. After three missed appointments in twelve months, we may ask for a deposit to book future visits.

## Exceptions

We waive the late cancellation fee for documented emergencies, severe weather closures announced by the clinic, and illness. Please let us know as soon as you can.
