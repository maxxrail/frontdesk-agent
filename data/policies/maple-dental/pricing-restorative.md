# Prices: Fillings, Crowns and Root Canals

## Fillings

White composite fillings start at $180 per surface. The final price depends on the tooth and the number of surfaces involved.

## Crowns

A porcelain crown is $1,350, including the temporary crown. Crowns usually need two visits about two weeks apart.

## Root canals

Root canal treatment ranges from $900 for a front tooth to $1,400 for a molar. A crown is often recommended after a root canal on a back tooth.
